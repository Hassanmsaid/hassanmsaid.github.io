Gravity Roll Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Gravity Roll (the "Game", Android package com.hassan.gravityroll, also available on iOS) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is developed and published by Hassan Said ("I", "me", "the developer"), who is responsible for your information. If you have any questions, email hassanmsaid5@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You don't need an account to play, and the Game never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Each install gets a random player ID instead (see Firebase Authentication below).
  • Your progress (levels, stars, daily streak, settings) is stored on your device only.
  • The Game uses Google services to measure how the Game is played (Firebase Analytics), to report crashes (Firebase Crashlytics), and to show rewarded ads (Google AdMob). These services collect device identifiers and usage data, as described below.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.
  • I do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your level progress, stars, best scores, attempts, daily challenge results and streak, and your settings (sound, language, reminders) in the app's local storage on your device. This data is never sent to me. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Information collected by third-party services

Firebase Authentication (Google)

When the Game first starts, it signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random player ID that is not linked to your name, email or any other account. Google stores the ID with the dates it was created and last used. The Game attaches this ID to analytics and crash reports so they can be grouped per player, and may use it in the future to save your progress online.

Firebase Analytics (Google)

I use Firebase Analytics to see how players progress through the Game, where they get stuck, and which features they use, so I can improve the levels and the Game. It collects:

  • Gameplay events, for example starting, finishing, retrying or quitting a level (with the level number, time taken, taps used and stars earned), daily challenge results, ad outcomes (whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end), settings changes, whether you allowed reminders, and opening the Game from a reminder.
  • Game summary values, such as your highest level reached, total stars, daily streak, chosen language and whether reminders are on.
  • Your player ID (see Firebase Authentication above).
  • Device and app information collected automatically by Firebase: an app instance ID, device model, operating system and version, app version, device language, approximate country or region (derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as part of your analytics data), session counts and duration, and, on Android, the advertising ID where available.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

If the Game crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics sends a crash report so I can fix it. A report contains technical details about the error (the stack trace), the device model, operating system, app version, the device state at the time (such as free memory and orientation), a Crashlytics installation ID, and your player ID. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game is free and supported by optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to restore tries). Google AdMob serves these ads and may collect:

  • the advertising ID (Android) and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, and
  • information about ad interactions (ads shown, clicked or watched).

Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud. On iOS, the Game does not ask for permission to track you, so the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is not available to the Game or to Google through it.

Google's privacy policies

Google processes this information as described in its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase privacy and security: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the Game asks your device for permission to show notifications and then schedules them locally on your device (for example, "today's daily challenge is ready"). No notification token or message content is sent to me or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the Game's settings or in your device settings at any time.

How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • run the Game and keep your progress on your device,
  • understand how the Game is played and improve its levels, difficulty and features,
  • find and fix crashes and bugs, and
  • show rewarded ads that keep the Game free, and measure how they perform.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, your information is processed on these legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests in measuring and improving the Game (analytics) and keeping it stable (crash reports).
  • Consent, where the law requires it, for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising, and for personalized ads. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Google, for Firebase and AdMob), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of players or the developer. I do not sell personal information for money.

Retention

  • Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.
  • Analytics data linked to an app instance or advertising ID is kept for no more than 14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Your player ID is kept by Firebase Authentication until you ask me to delete it.
  • AdMob data is kept according to Google's policies linked above.

Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. On iOS, the Game does not use your IDFA.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.
  • Start fresh: uninstalling the Game deletes your local progress and resets the app instance ID used by Firebase Analytics. Reinstalling gives you a new player ID.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, object to or restrict the processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because the Game has no accounts, I can't identify you from your name or email, so please include your device type and describe your request. Email hassanmsaid5@gmail.com and I will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA), the categories of personal information collected in the last 12 months are: identifiers (player ID, app instance ID, advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic activity (gameplay and ad interactions), and approximate geolocation (country or region). They come from your device, for the purposes described above. I do not sell personal information for money. Letting Google use the advertising ID to personalize ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out by deleting or resetting your advertising ID as described above, or by emailing me. I will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, email hassanmsaid5@gmail.com and I will take steps to delete it.

Security

Information sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but I use reputable providers and collect only what the Game needs.

International transfers

Google may process information in the United States and other countries where it or its subprocessors operate. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when new features such as cloud saves or in-app purchases are added, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes, I will also give notice in the Game.
